# Exchangers & Traders

These NPCs let you **trade** items, coins, and Zeny for useful rewards — or turn your Zeny and materials into the **tokens** used by the [Gachapon](gachapon.md) machines.

## Item Collector

Turn unwanted items into **Zeny** in a flash. Just pick an item and how many you want to sell.

**Location:** Prontera 147, 234

## Bounty Card Coin Exchange

A reward shop where you spend your **Card Coins** (and sometimes a little Zeny) on special items.

**Location:** Prontera 120, 158

## 4bMMO Exchanger

A reward shop where you turn in **Zeny, points, and materials** for special gear.

**Location:** Prontera 164, 246

## BOUNCE! Medals

- **BOUNCE! Medal Maker** (Prontera 189, 209): converts **4 BOUNCE! Tokens → 1 Bounce! Medal**.

## Token Makers

Turn your Zeny (or items) into the tokens that the [Gachapon](gachapon.md) machines use:

| Token Maker | Location | Exchange rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mega Shadow Token Maker | Prontera 137, 222 | 58,000,000 Zeny → 1 Mega Shadow Token |
| BOUNCE! Token Maker | Prontera 189, 198 | 188,000,888 Zeny (or a Bounty Vaccine Cure Stone, or Bounty Coin) → 1 BOUNCE! Token |
| Bountiersary Token Maker | Prontera 143, 228 | 138,888,888 Zeny → 1 Bountiersary Token |
