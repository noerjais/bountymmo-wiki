# Paladin — Skill Formulas

The **Paladin** is the transcendent Swordman → Crusader branch: the ultimate **tank and protector**, wielding shields and **Holy** power to guard allies and punish the undead.

Values below are the **exact ratios from this server's code**, applied to your ATK (or MATK for Holy magic) before DEF/element — see [Damage & Combat Formulas](damage-formulas.md).

## Damage skills

| Skill | Damage (by skill level) | Max level | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Rapid Smiting** *(Shield Chain)* | 100% + 30% × Lv | Lv5 → **250%** | Ranged shield throw; also scales with your **shield's refine and weight**. |
| **Shield Boomerang** | 100% + 30% × Lv | Lv5 → **250%** | Throws your shield at a target. |
| **Shield Charge** | 100% + 20% × Lv | Lv5 → **200%** | Rush a target; chance to **Stun**. |
| **Holy Cross** | 100% + 35% × Lv (**+70% × Lv with a two-handed spear**) | Lv10 → **450% / 800%** | Holy-element strike. |
| **Grand Cross** | Combined physical + magic damage × (100% + 40% × Lv) | Lv10 → **×5** | Holy area blast — also hits **you**, so watch your HP. |
| **Martyr's Reckoning** *(Sacrifice)* | 90% + 10% × Lv per hit | Lv5 → **140%** | Powered by **your own HP** — each hit spends some of your life. |
| **Pressure** | Fixed heavy damage that **ignores DEF** | — | Also drains the target's SP; can't miss. |

## Key defensive & support skills

- **Devotion** — take the damage aimed at your allies (the core "bodyguard" skill).
- **Auto Guard** — chance to block incoming attacks with your shield.
- **Reflect Shield** — bounce a portion of melee damage back to attackers.
- **Defending Aura** — big reduction to **ranged** physical damage (for you and nearby allies).
- **Providence** — resistance vs Holy element and Demi-Human attacks.
- **Battle Chant (Gospel)** — random powerful party buffs and enemy debuffs.
- **Shield Reflect / Spear Quicken / Faith** — extra shield and spear utility.
