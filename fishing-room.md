# Fishing Room

There are spots into this room where you can fish. They are recognizable and they are like these:

![Fishing zone 1](.gitbook/assets/Fishing_zone_1.png)

![Fishing zone 2](.gitbook/assets/Fishing_zone_2.png)

The only things to have in your inventory are ![King Earthworm](.gitbook/assets/King_Earthworm.png)[King Earthworm](currency-list.md#king-earthworm), then clicking the bubbles you start the fishing.

You don't need any finishing gears to fish. Fishing gears only decreases the fishing time.

**Tip:** Don't sell the Fishes to NPC since it's required for Questing, and as well very demanded in the market!
