# Useful NPCs & Rooms

A handy roundup of the helper NPCs and special rooms around town.

## Special Enchanter

Adds powerful enchantments to your gear. For the basics of enchanting, check the guides: [Payon Enchant](payon-enchant.md), [Socket Enchant](socket-enchant.md), and [Malangdo Enchant](malangdo-enchant.md).

**Location:** itemmall 193, 57

## MvP Card Recycler

Got duplicate **MvP Cards**? Trade in a set number of them for **1 special item** — a great way to turn spare cards into something you'll actually use.

**Location:** Prontera 171, 225

## Bounty Garden

Your very own little farm! **Plant** seeds, **water** them as they grow through their stages, and **harvest** them for rewards once they're ready. Visit the garden and tend your crops over time.

**Location:** Prontera 164, 164 (entrance) — the garden itself is on its own farm map.

## Monthly Reward

Claim a **free reward once every month** — it resets on the 1st, so don't forget to come back.

**Location:** Prontera 162, 127

## Premium Storage

Opens an **extended storage** for extra space (you'll need an Extended Storage Ticket).

**Location:** Prontera 183, 193
