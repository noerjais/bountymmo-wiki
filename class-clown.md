# Clown — Skill Formulas

The **Clown** (Minstrel) is the transcendent Archer → Bard branch (male). It fights with a bow and, above all, buffs the whole party with powerful **songs**.

Values below are the **exact ratios from this server's code** — see [Damage & Combat Formulas](damage-formulas.md).

## Damage skills

| Skill | Damage (by skill level) | Max level | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Arrow Vulcan** | 200% + 100% × Lv | Lv10 → **1200%** | The Clown's big burst — a rapid volley of arrows. Requires being in a song state. |
| **Musical Strike** | 125% + 25% × Lv | Lv5 → **250%** | Ranged hit using an instrument. |
| **Tarot Card of Fate** | Random | — | Draws a card for a random powerful effect — from heavy damage to strong debuffs (Freeze, Stun, gear break, HP/SP drain…). A gamble every time. |

## Songs (solo)

Songs play in an area around you and buff allies (or debuff enemies):

- **Poem of Bragi (The Poem of Bragi)** — reduces cast time and after-cast delay. The most wanted party buff in the game.
- **Assassin Cross of Sunset** — boosts ASPD.
- **A Whistle** — boosts FLEE and Perfect Dodge.
- **Apple of Idun** — raises Max HP and heals over time.
- **Impressive Riff / Magic Strings (Song of Lutie)** — attack and magic support.
- **Frost Joke (Unbarring Octave)** — chance to Freeze nearby enemies.
- **Dissonance (Ugly Dance)** — a song that **damages** enemies in the area over time.

## Ensembles (need a Bard + Dancer together)

- **Loki's Veil (Classical Pluck)** — blocks skills in an area.
- **Longing for Freedom** — lets you move/act during ensembles.
- **Marionette Control** — transfer half your stats to a partner.
