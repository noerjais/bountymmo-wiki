# Seasonal Events

Throughout the year Bounty MMO runs special limited-time celebrations. They only appear during their season, so keep an eye on the in-game announcements and Discord to catch them. Here's what to expect.

## Anniversary Events

The server's birthday is the biggest celebration of the year, and each anniversary brings its own events.

### 5th Anniversary

- **Bountiful Anniver5ary** (Prontera 149, 207): exchange **1 "5th Anniversary Grace Coin"** for a gacha pull that's **100% guaranteed** to give one of the exclusive costumes.
- **5th Anniversary Dungeon** — enter through the **Gate Guardian** (Prontera 134, 203). Inside are **7 ancient Stone Circles** spelling out **B · O · U · N · T · Y · 5**. Hunt the Bounty Monsters for **Sign Coins**, then deposit **18 of the correct coins** into each circle. Once all 7 circles are charged, the boss **Keeper of the Fifth** awakens — defeat it for glory! (Each day a different monster spawns more often, dropping specific coins.)
- Nearby helpers: **Sign Converter**, **5th Anniversary Exchanger**, **Trader**, and **Gachapon** (all around Prontera, 119–131 / 199–209).

### 4th Anniversary

- **Annivers4ry Wonders** (Prontera 177, 219): play with an anniversary token for a random prize.
- **4th Anniversary Gachapon** (port 153, 328): spins for special anniversary rewards.

### 3rd Anniversary

- **Bounty Merchant / Redeem** (Prontera 146, 231): turn in anniversary tokens for rewards like the **3rd Anniversary Rings and Earrings**.
- **Anniversary Raid** — a wave-defense battle (kicked off by **Phoenix**) where you protect the venue from waves of monsters and bosses.

## Chinese New Year

- **CNY Redeem** and **CNY Gachapon** machines appear in Prontera (around 143–147 / 237–243) with festive rewards.
- The newest run, **CNY 2026**, adds the **Flaming** and **Blazing Gachapon** plus a fresh reward shop (Prontera, 143–164 / 246–250).

## Bountyween (Halloween)

The spooky-season event, **Bountyween**, brings a haunted map, seasonal monster spawns, and the **Hauntyween Exchange** (Prontera 147, 249) where you trade your Halloween loot for treats.

## Midgard's World Tree (Community Support)

A **server-wide community goal**. Everyone can chip in — donate **event items, Zeny, or Cash Points** — to fill a shared progress bar. As the total climbs, new **reward stages (tiers)** unlock for the whole server, so every bit helps. Check the World Tree to see the current progress and what unlocks next.

**Location:** port 153, 332
