# Endless Tower

A classic party challenge: a **101-floor** tower packed with monsters and bosses. Fight your way up floor by floor — the higher you climb, the tougher (and more rewarding) it gets. Powerful **MVP bosses** guard certain floors, so bring a solid party and plenty of supplies.

## How it works

- **You need a party** — form one and have the **party leader** reserve the entrance.
- Clear the monsters on each floor to open the way up, then keep climbing toward the top.
- Your party gets its own private copy of the tower, so no one else can interfere with your run.

## Difficulty modes

Choose your challenge when you enter. Tougher modes give **bigger EXP rewards**:

| Mode | Level requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Normal | Level 50+ |
| Veteran | Level 100+ |
| Nightmare | Level 100+ |
| Hell | Level 100+ |
| **Torment** | Level 100+ — the ultimate challenge |

Every party member must meet the level requirement for the mode you pick.

## How to enter

Head to the Endless Tower from the **Warper**, then talk to the **Tower Protection Stone** (e_tower 81, 105). The party leader can reserve the dungeon and warp the team inside from there. (The same NPC can also send you back to Alberta.)
