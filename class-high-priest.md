# High Priest — Skill Formulas

The **High Priest** is the transcendent Acolyte → Priest branch: the game's best **support and healer**, with strong **Holy** damage against Undead and Demon monsters.

Holy damage is MATK-based (see [Damage & Combat Formulas](damage-formulas.md)). The ratios below come from this server's code.

## Damage skills

| Skill | Element | Damage | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Magnus Exorcismus** | Holy | Multiple Holy hits over an area | Shreds **Undead and Demon** monsters — the Priest's main damage tool. |
| **Turn Undead** | Holy | Chance to **instantly kill** an Undead monster; otherwise deals Holy damage | Kill chance depends on your level, INT and LUK vs the target. |
| **Holy Light** | Holy | 125% MATK | Quick single Holy bolt. |
| **Mace attacks** | — | Normal ATK, boosted by **Mace Mastery** | Battle-priest builds hit surprisingly hard. |

## Lex Aeterna

A key skill: it marks a target so the **next hit it takes deals double damage**. Priests use it to set up their own Magnus Exorcismus or to amplify an ally's burst — one of the most valuable support skills in the game.

## Key support skills

- **Heal** — restores HP (and damages Undead); scales with INT and base level.
- **Sanctuary** — an area that heals allies and hurts Undead standing in it.
- **Assumptio** — halves the physical and magic damage an ally takes.
- **Kyrie Eleison** — a barrier that blocks a number of hits.
- **Increase AGI / Blessing** — the classic party buffs (ASPD/movement, and STR/INT/DEX + hit).
- **Magnificat** — boosts SP regen for the whole party.
- **Gloria** — big LUK boost.
- **Impositio Manus** — adds flat ATK to an ally's weapon.
- **Safety Wall / Pneuma** — block melee / block ranged on a tile.
- **Resurrection** — revive fallen allies.
- **Aspersio** — endows a weapon with the **Holy** element.
- **Lex Divina** — Silences a target.
