# Stalker — Skill Formulas

The **Stalker** is the transcendent Thief → Rogue branch: a sneaky disruptor that **strips enemy gear** and can **copy other classes' skills**.

Values below are the **exact ratios from this server's code**, applied to your ATK before DEF/element — see [Damage & Combat Formulas](damage-formulas.md).

## Damage skills

| Skill | Damage (by skill level) | Max level | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Back Stab** | 300% + 40% × Lv | Lv10 → **700%** | Must hit from **behind**. Using a **bow** halves the damage. |
| **Raid** | 100% + 20% × Lv (vs bosses: 100% + 10% × Lv) | Lv5 → **200%** | Area follow-up after **Sightless Mind** (an ambush); adds a short debuff. |
| **Intimidate** | 100% + 30% × Lv | Lv5 → **250%** | Hits and warps you + the target a short distance. |

## Plagiarism — copy any skill

The Stalker's trademark. With **Plagiarism** you **copy the last skill an enemy used on you**, and **Preserve** lets you **keep** that copied skill permanently (instead of losing it when you learn a new one). A Stalker can steal and keep powerful skills like **Asura Strike, Storm Gust, Sonic Blow, or Meteor Storm** — so a Stalker's real damage often comes from whatever it has copied.

## Strip & disruption

- **Divest Weapon / Armor / Shield / Helm** — rip a single piece of gear off an enemy.
- **Full Strip** — remove **all** of a target's equipment at once.
- **Sightless Mind** — the ambush that sets up **Raid**.
- **Reject Sword** — reflect part of an attacker's melee damage.
- **Close Confine** — lock yourself and a target together in melee.

## Mobility & thievery

- **Stealth / Chase Walk** — move unseen (Chase Walk even boosts your speed).
- **Backslide** — dash backward instantly.
- **Steal / Mug / Gank** — pull extra loot and Zeny from enemies.
