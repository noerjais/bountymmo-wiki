# Infinite Space

This instance has 50 floors, you can just progress easily by killing the mobs on each floor (like Endless Cellar and Endless Tower) The MVPs drop unique cards that are worth hunting.

The monsters have a lot of HP so it can be a little time consuming to complete. Depending on your gear, choose normal mode.

**Infinite Space** **Monsters**

###### Hard Mode:

You can get a chance to randomly encounter

- Infinite Toad
- Infinite Vagabond Wolf
- Infinite Vocal
- Infinite Eclipse
- Infinite Chimera<br />

The "Infinite" MVP Cards are worth hunting for.

Example:

- Inifinite Phreeoni Card can add + 100 Crit (weapon slot)
- Infinite Orc Hero Card has 100% chance to stun attackers (headgear slot)

- Infinite Chimera (on hard mode) is a garment card that can MaxHP +8%, MaxSP +4%, but when combined with other can add another 10% ATK & 10% MATK

The smaller MVPs (on hard mode) like Infinite Vagabond Wolf (footgear card) can add ATK +10. If refined to +7, ATK +10. If refined to +9, ATK +15.

Happy hunting!
