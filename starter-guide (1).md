# starter guide

While the wiki is a work in progress, please also check the resources and guides in the Bounty Discord. Check the pinned messages or use search.

Some basic questions commonly asked by newcomers are below. If you have more questions or need information and clarification, feel free to post in the Bounty Discord and we'll be happy to answer them. Everyone is encouraged to explore the server in-game and on Discord.

## Where to Start

Everyone has their favorite job they prefer to use. It's easy to level up in-game.

You can use Battle Manual or Battle Manual 3x to help you level faster. You may also use the Crystals at Prontera to AFK level; you only need to auto attack.

A character for farming is essential to help farm zeny, equipment, cards, and quest materials.

## Newbie Freebies

When you are in-game, write the following in chat:

```
@request "New Family Bountiers here"
```

If a GM is online and you are confirmed to be a newbie and have met the requirement, a GM will give you other freebies.

If a GM does not contact you in-game because they are offline, try again later. GMs are often online to give support to players.
