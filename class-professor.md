# Professor — Skill Formulas

The **Professor** is the transcendent Mage → Sage branch: a **control and anti-mage** specialist. It casts the same elemental spells as the Wizard line, but shines at shutting down enemy casters and protecting its party.

Magic damage is MATK-based — see [Damage & Combat Formulas](damage-formulas.md).

## Damage skills

- **Elemental Bolts** — Professors use the same **Fire / Cold / Lightning Bolt**, **Earth Spike**, and **Heaven's Drive** as the [High Wizard](class-high-wizard.md); each bolt is **100% MATK per hit** (one hit per skill level). Match the element to your target.
- **Soul Burn** — deals damage equal to **twice the target's current SP**, and can **burn away all of their SP**. Brutal against other spellcasters — the more SP they have, the more it hurts.
- **Fire Bolt via Auto Spell** — Professors can auto-cast bolts while meleeing (see below).

## Control & anti-mage toolkit

- **Dispell** — removes almost all buffs from a target.
- **Spell Breaker** — cancels an enemy's cast and steals their SP.
- **Mind Breaker** — lowers a target's MDEF while raising their MATK (set up a kill).
- **Spider Web** — roots a target and makes them take extra **Fire** damage.
- **Fiber Lock** — reinforces Spider Web.
- **Land Protector** — blocks all ground-targeted skills (Storm Gust, Sanctuary, traps…) in an area.
- **Magnetic Earth** — same idea, protects a tile.
- **Volcano / Deluge / Violent Gale** — elemental fields that boost Fire / Water / Wind power and HP.

## Caster utility

- **Free Cast** — move and attack while casting.
- **Memorize** — halve the cast time of your next spells.
- **Auto Spell** — randomly auto-cast a chosen bolt when you attack.
- **Soul Change** — swap your SP total with an ally's.
- **Health Conversion** — turn HP into SP.
- **Double Casting** — chance to instantly cast a bolt twice.
