# Assassin Cross — Skill Formulas

The **Assassin Cross** is the transcendent Thief → Assassin branch: a lethal dual-wield / katar killer famous for poison, burst damage, and **Enchant Deadly Poison**.

Every damage skill starts from a **base of 100%** and adds a bonus on top. The values below are the **exact skill ratios used on this server**. This % applies to your ATK; the enemy's DEF, element, size, and your cards still apply afterward — see [Damage & Combat Formulas](damage-formulas.md).

## Damage skills

| Skill | Damage (by skill level) | Max level | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sonic Blow** | 400% + 40% × Lv | Lv10 → **800%** | The signature combo — many rapid hits. **Sonic Acceleration** adds +50% accuracy to it. |
| **Grimtooth** | 100% + 20% × Lv | Lv5 → **200%** | Can be used while **Cloaked**; its range grows with level. |
| **Poison React** | 100% + 30% × Lv | Lv5 → **250%** | Counter-attack that punishes Poison-element enemies. |
| **Meteor Assault** | 40% + 40% × Lv | Lv10 → **440%** | Area burst around you; can cause Bleeding, Stun, or Blind. |
| **Venom Splasher** | 500% + 50% × Lv (**+20% per Poison React level**) | Lv10 → **1000%+** | Plants a "poison bomb" on a target that explodes for huge area damage. |
| **Soul Breaker** *(Soul Destroyer)* | Physical part: 100% × Lv, **plus** a separate magic hit that scales with INT/MATK | Lv5 → **500%** physical + magic | Ranged strike that mixes **physical and magic** damage — hard to defend against. |
| **Envenom** | ~100% (Poison element) | — | Small guaranteed Poison-element hit that can inflict **Poison**. |

> **How to read this:** e.g. Sonic Blow Lv10 = 100% + 300% + 40%×10 = **800%** of your ATK total (spread over its many hits), before the enemy's DEF and your element/size/card modifiers.

## Enchant Deadly Poison (EDP)

**EDP** is the Assassin Cross's trademark. While active it **hugely increases your attack** (poison-element damage), turning your normal attacks and Sonic Blow into massive hits. It's expensive (uses Deadly Poison Bottles) and time-limited, so it's saved for bosses and burst windows. On this server EDP uses the **classic** strong multiplier.

## Key buffs & utility

- **Katar Mastery / Advanced Katar Mastery** — more damage with katar weapons.
- **Enchant Poison** — adds Poison element to your attacks.
- **Sonic Acceleration** — boosts Sonic Blow's accuracy (see above).
- **Cloaking** — move unseen next to walls; enables Grimtooth.
- **Create Deadly Poison** — craft the Deadly Poison Bottles that fuel EDP.
- **Right-Hand / Left-Hand Mastery** — improves dual-wield damage.
