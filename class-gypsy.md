# Gypsy — Skill Formulas

The **Gypsy** is the transcendent Archer → Dancer branch (female): the mirror of the [Clown](class-clown.md). She fights with a bow/whip and supports the party with **dances**.

Values below are the **exact ratios from this server's code** — see [Damage & Combat Formulas](damage-formulas.md).

## Damage skills

| Skill | Damage (by skill level) | Max level | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Arrow Vulcan** | 200% + 100% × Lv | Lv10 → **1200%** | The Gypsy's big burst — a rapid volley of arrows. Requires being in a dance state. |
| **Throw Arrow (Slinging Arrow)** | 125% + 25% × Lv | Lv5 → **250%** | Ranged hit. |
| **Tarot Card of Fate** | Random | — | Draws a card for a random powerful effect — heavy damage or strong debuffs (Freeze, Stun, gear break, HP/SP drain…). A gamble every time. |
| **Scream** | — | — | Area shout with a chance to **Stun** nearby enemies. |

## Dances (solo)

Dances play in an area around you and buff allies (or debuff enemies):

- **Fortune's Kiss (A Poem of Bragi's partner)** — boosts Critical rate.
- **Service for You** — raises Max SP and reduces SP costs.
- **Humming** — boosts HIT.
- **Please Don't Forget Me** — slows enemies' movement and ASPD.
- **Lady Luck (Gypsy's Kiss)** — luck/So support.
- **Hip Shaker / Dance** — SP support and control.

## Ensembles (need a Bard + Dancer together)

- **Loki's Veil (Classical Pluck)** — blocks skills in an area.
- **Longing for Freedom** — lets you move/act during ensembles.
- **Marionette Control** — transfer half your stats to a partner.
