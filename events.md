# Events

Here you can find information about all events on this server.

## Devil Square Event

![Devil Square NPC](.gitbook/assets/Devil_Square_NPC.gif)

Click the NPC at Prontera (156,149) when it announces that Devil Square is about to start

**Activation time:** At 00:00 and every 2 hours.

**Reward:** x1 [Devil Square Token](currency-list.md#devil-square-token) - x10 [Poring Coin](currency-list.md#poring-coin)

## Mushroom Event

![Mushroom Event NPC](.gitbook/assets/Mushroom_Event_NPC.gif)

In the public chat you can read the announcement that warns you where the mushrooms have spawned, they could be at Izlude or Geffen. Check NPC at Prontera (167,139) to see if any on going event is active.

**Activation time:** If all mushroom has been killed, at 00:00 and every hour

**Reward for every mushroom found:** x1 [Mushroom Finder Token](currency-list.md#mushroom-finder-token) - x1 [Bounty **Coin**](currency-list.md#bounty-coin)

## Mr. Dice Event

![Event teleport](.gitbook/assets/Event_teleport.gif)

When it is active, there is a teleport in Prontera Town **(insert coordinate here)** that teleport you into a special room. When the game start, as say also the narrator, you must choose a box from 1 to 4 where stay. A number is thrown and if it matches the box you are in, you win. The numbers are drawn on the ground with tiles. In case there are more players in different box, if you lose, you instantly teleported to Prontera Town. The game finish when only a player remain.

**Activation time:**

**Reward:** x1 [Dice Beater Token](currency-list.md#dice-beater-token) - x10 [Poring Coin](currency-list.md#poring-coin)

If the time expire and the monsters won, you can anyway claim your reward if you have killed at least a monster. The NPC to reclaim your reward will spawn at Prontera Town **(insert coordinate here).** Beware, it despawn in 5 min.

**Activation time:**
