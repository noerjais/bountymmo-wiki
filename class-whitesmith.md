# Whitesmith — Skill Formulas

The **Whitesmith** is the transcendent Merchant → Blacksmith branch: a sturdy melee damage dealer (axes and maces) and the game's master **weapon forger**.

Values below are the **exact ratios from this server's code**, applied to your ATK before DEF/element/size — see [Damage & Combat Formulas](damage-formulas.md).

## Damage skills

| Skill | Damage (by skill level) | Max level | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Mammonite** | 100% + 50% × Lv | Lv10 → **600%** | Reliable single hit; spends a little **Zeny** each cast. |
| **Cart Revolution** | **150%** | — | Area hit that knocks enemies back — needs a Cart. |
| **Cart Termination** | Scales with your **Cart Weight** and skill level | very high | The heavier your loaded cart (and the higher the level), the bigger the hit — a fully packed cart can deal enormous damage. Costs Zeny and a Spirit Sphere. |

> **Cart Termination tip:** because damage grows with cart weight, Whitesmiths deliberately fill their cart before big fights. Keep it heavy for maximum burst.

## Key buffs & utility

- **Adrenaline Rush** — big ASPD boost for axes/maces (also affects nearby party members).
- **Power Thrust** — adds a flat % of extra ATK (party-wide).
- **Weapon Perfection** — removes the size penalty, so you deal full damage to any size.
- **Maximize Power** — forces your weapon to always roll its **maximum** ATK (drains SP over time).
- **Over Thrust / Over Thrust Max** — extra damage at the cost of some weapon durability risk.
- **Cart Boost** — move faster with a cart equipped.
- **Melt Down** — chance to **break the enemy's weapon or armor**.
- **Weaponsmithing / Forging** — craft weapons (and Whitesmiths forge the best ones, including elemental and star-crumb weapons).
