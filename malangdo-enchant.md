# Malangdo Enchant

### **How it works:**

- Talk with [Mayo Mayo](prontera-town.md#mayo-mayo) NPC on Prontera
- 1x [Seagod Anger](special-ingredients-and-objects.md#seagod-anger) per enchantment
- No chance of failure
- Only level 4 weapons can be enchanted
