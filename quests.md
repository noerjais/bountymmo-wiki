# Quests

Beyond the main game, Bounty MMO has special quest NPCs where you turn in materials for powerful gear — plus endgame challenges made for max-level heroes.

## Godly Quest (God Items)

The endgame crafting line for legendary **God items**. It all starts with the **Godly Warper**, who sends you to the **Dragon Castle** — the home base of the quest.

**Where to start:** Godly Warper @ 1@lhz 138, 98 (it warps you into Dragon Castle).

Inside Dragon Castle you'll work with these NPCs:

- **Gliepnir Seal**
- **Godly Stone Maker**
- **Demi God**
- **The Real Deal**

Work through them to gather the materials and craft your God item. Bring a strong, well-geared character — Dragon Castle is no place for the unprepared.

## Exalted Collector

A reward shop where you trade **Zeny, points, and rare materials** for exalted gear.

**Location:** 1@lhz 159, 134

## New Family — "Journey Begins"

A quest exchange where you redeem special rewards for meeting its requirements.

**Location:** quiz_02 147, 382

## Proof of Solidarity

Turn in the required materials here to claim its reward.

**Location:** quiz_02 133, 386

## Ultimate End Game

The final challenge, open only to **Base Level 175** heroes. Step through the gate to face the ultimate test — only true fighters are allowed inside.

**Location:** quiz_02 170, 392 *(requires Base Level 175)*
