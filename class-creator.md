# Creator — Skill Formulas

The **Creator** (Biochemist) is the transcendent Merchant → Alchemist branch: a **potion-throwing, MVP-slaying** specialist that also fights with Homunculus pets.

Values below are the **exact ratios from this server's code** — see [Damage & Combat Formulas](damage-formulas.md) for how they turn into final damage.

## Damage skills

| Skill | Element | Damage | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Acid Demonstration** | Neutral (final) | Damage **scales with the target's VIT and DEF** | The signature skill — the tankier the target, the harder it hits. **Devastating against MVPs and high-VIT players.** Uses ATK **and** MATK, so both stats matter. |
| **Acid Terror** | — | 300% + 80% × Lv (Lv5 → **700%**) | Throws acid bottles; chance to **break armor**. |
| **Demonstration (Bomb)** | Fire | 100% + 20% × Lv (Lv5 → **200%**) | Chance to **break the enemy's weapon**. |

> **Acid Demonstration:** because it feeds on the enemy's own VIT and DEF, it's the go-to MVP killer. It needs thrown **Acid Bottles**, so keep a stock crafted.

## Homunculus

Creators raise a **Homunculus** companion (Lif, Amistr, Filir, or Vanilmirth) that levels up, learns its own skills, and fights beside you. Each type fills a role — tank, damage, or support caster.

## Key skills & utility

- **Potion Pitcher / Slim Potion Pitcher** — throw healing potions onto allies (a Creator can out-heal many supports).
- **Full Chemical Protection** — shields an ally's weapon, armor, shield, and helm from being stripped or broken.
- **Bio Cannibalize** — summon plant monsters to fight for you.
- **Sphere Mine / Cultivation** — deploy explosives and grow plants/mushrooms.
- **Chemical Weapon/Status potions** — craft acid bottles, bottle grenades, and buff potions.
- **Axe Mastery / Cart skills** — inherited Merchant/Blacksmith power for melee builds.
