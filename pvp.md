# PvP Arenas

Love fighting other players? Bounty MMO has a couple of ways to test your skills.

## PvP Arena

An enhanced arena that tracks your **Kills, Deaths, and Assists**, complete with rankings and rewards. Choose your battle mode and jump into the action. (Novices can't enter — pick a job first.)

**How to join:** talk to the **PvP Warper** in Prontera (164, 142).

## X Coliseum

A free-for-all PvP map — warp in and battle anyone who dares.
