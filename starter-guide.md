# Starter Guide

While wiki is in WIP, please check also the resources and guide that has been made in the Bounty Discord. Please either check the pinned messages or try to search. Some of the basic, usually asked questions by newbies are below. If you have more questions, needs more information and clarifications, please feel free to post your questions in the Bounty Discord and we'll be happy to answer them. Everyone is encouraged to explore the server in-game and in discord. If you have any question, please feel free to ask at Bounty Discord.

#### Where to Start

Everyone has their favorite job they prefer to use. It's easy to level up in-game. You can use Battle Manual or Battle Manual 3x to help you make it faster. You may also use the Crystals at Prontera to AFK level, you only need to auto attack. A character for farming is essential to help farm zeny, equipment, cards and quest materials.

#### Newbie Freebies

When you are in game write in chat **@request "New Family Bountiers here"**. If a GM is online and you are confirmed to be a newbie and have met the requirement, a GM will give you other Freebies. In case GM do not contact you in game because is offline, try again later. GMs are often online to give support to the players
